How a random trader is drawn
  side            coin toss, redrawn until both sides are present
  start amount L  one of 50k, 100k, 200k, 500k in the trader's own currency (the four cards' amounts)
  target T        all of L converted at 1.25, times u; u uniform in 0.90-1.10
  penalties A, B  each one of 0%, 2%, 3%, 5%, 10%, 20%
  market          4 rounds, rate opens at 1.25 $/GBP and moves by a normal step of sd 0.05 a round
